Traditional Chinese gongbi meticulous-brush painting style, an explicitly adult East Asian woman in her late twenties wearing a pale celadon-green cross-collar hanfu with wide flowing sleeves and a long pleated skirt, standing in a classical Jiangnan garden beside a whitewashed wall and a plum branch heavy with blossom. She is captured from behind at three-quarter back view, her back and the long fall of her hair toward the camera, her head turned back over her left shoulder so her face is seen in three-quarter profile with the chin rotated toward the lens, eyes glancing back toward the camera, one hand lifting the edge of her sleeve near her collar. Medium-full figure framing, camera at shoulder height, slight three-quarter rear viewpoint. Delicate gongbi linework with fine layered shading, mineral pigment palette of malachite green, azurite blue and warm ochre, gold-thread highlights along the collar, scattered plum petals on the ground, soft misty depth behind the wall where a stone lantern and bamboo stand, rice-paper texture, elegant restrained composition.
